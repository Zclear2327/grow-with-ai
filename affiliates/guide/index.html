What to say and not say

Be specific, be honest, never promise money.

Say

  • What the person actually gets: the format, the length and what they leave with
  • Who it's for, and who it isn't for
  • Your own honest experience, if you've used a package
  • That you earn a commission (see the next section)
  • That they keep control: anything that spends money waits for their approval

Never say

  • Any promise of income, profit, savings or hours saved ("you'll make $10k", "save 20 hours a week")
  • That AI will replace their staff
  • Fake urgency or scarcity ("only 2 seats left", "price goes up tonight") unless we've told you it's true
  • Testimonials or client names we haven't given you
  • That Grow With AI is part of, partnered with, or endorsed by Anthropic
  • Anything presented as if you are Grow With AI or Chris

Why so strict: Grow With AI's own pages promise no results, revenue, savings or income, and US law (FTC) holds both you and us responsible for what you claim.
