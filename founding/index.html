How it works

The Ward 5, then 5 agents.

You start with The Ward 5, our short intake with your AI trainer, Sam or Travis. It turns into your own agent plan. Then you set up 5 agents, one at a time. Each one drafts, and you approve.

  1. 1

    Email & Inbox Agent

    Sorts your inbox, flags what needs you today, and drafts replies for you to send.

  2. 2

    Customer Questions Agent

    Answers the questions customers ask most, from your own information, and hands the tricky ones to you.

  3. 3

    Sales & Follow-Up Agent

    Keeps track of leads, drafts follow-ups and quotes, and reminds you.

  4. 4

    Daily Assistant

    Your morning brief: calendar, to-dos, bookings and errands.

  5. 5

    Content & Social Agent

    Drafts posts, newsletters and short video scripts in your voice for you to approve.
