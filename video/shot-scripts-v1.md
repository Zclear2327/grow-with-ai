# Shot scripts v1: two demo videos for the home page

For JR to record. 30 to 60 seconds each. Caption on both: **"Simple, and you stay in charge."**

Rules for both videos:
- No income, savings or time-saved numbers, spoken or on screen.
- No real card, passport, password or account details on screen. Use demo data and blur anything that looks real.
- Show the approval tap clearly and hold on it for at least 2 seconds.
- The agent never sends, pays or books by itself. Every action waits for a yes.
